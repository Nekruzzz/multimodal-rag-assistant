# Field sensors

Place soil moisture probes at two depths, about 15 cm and 30 cm, so you can see whether water reaches the deeper roots. Install them in representative spots, away from field edges and irrigation emitters.

Calibrate each probe against a gravimetric sample at the start of the season, because readings differ between soil types. Recalibrate after replacing a sensor.

Log readings at least every 30 minutes. A gateway that sends data over LoRa or Wi-Fi can batch the readings and upload them hourly to save battery.

Treat sudden jumps or flat lines as likely sensor faults. Compare with neighbouring probes before acting on a reading.
