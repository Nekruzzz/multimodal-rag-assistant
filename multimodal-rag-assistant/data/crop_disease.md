# Leaf blight and early disease detection

Leaf blight shows up as brown or tan lesions on leaves, often with a yellow halo. Lesions usually start on the lower leaves and spread upward as the disease progresses.

Blight spreads fastest when leaves stay wet for many hours and the air is humid and warm. Keep plants spaced so air can move between them, and avoid watering the leaves in the evening.

Check a few plants in each row every week. Photograph affected leaves with a fixed camera angle and consistent lighting so changes over time are easy to compare. Remove badly infected leaves, and do not compost them.

If more than ten percent of leaves in a field section show lesions, treat that section with a fungicide approved for the crop and follow the label interval.
