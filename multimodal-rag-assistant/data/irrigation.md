# Irrigation scheduling

Drip irrigation delivers water slowly to the root zone and wastes far less than overhead sprinklers. Schedule it from soil moisture readings instead of the calendar.

Start irrigation when volumetric soil moisture falls below about 30 percent for loamy soil, and stop when it reaches roughly 40 percent. Sandy soil drains faster, so use shorter and more frequent cycles. Clay soil holds water longer, so water less often but for longer.

Irrigate early in the morning. Evaporation is lowest, wind is calm, and leaves dry before evening, which lowers the chance of fungal disease. Avoid watering at midday in hot weather.

After heavy rain, skip the next scheduled cycle and check the sensors again before restarting.
